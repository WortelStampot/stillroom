# Stillroom

A quiet viewer for public-domain paintings, drawings and prints from the Art Institute of Chicago and the Rijksmuseum.
One work at a time, no name attached until you ask for it.

- **Live:** https://wortelstampot.github.io/stillroom/
- **Dev:** https://wortelstampot.github.io/stillroom/dev/

## Keys

→ / Space next · ← back · K keep · I label · scroll or pinch to zoom · drag to move · G glide ·
Esc or 0 whole work · P kept pile · F full screen · H keep controls showing · D save session log (every session since the last D)

## Images and data

Images and records come from the [Art Institute of Chicago API](https://api.artic.edu/docs) and its IIIF image server,
and from the [Rijksmuseum's data services](https://data.rijksmuseum.nl/) (no key) and its IIIF image server at iiif.micr.io.
The top bar picks one museum or both (about half from each). Only works the museums mark as public domain are shown.

`tools/stillroom.command` sets the Mac's screen for viewing (see comments inside).
